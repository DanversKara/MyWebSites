1. What we collect

In plain language: the website collects almost nothing, the app sends nothing to us, and your PBX account holds what a phone line needs to work.

This website

OwnPBX.org is a static site: no tracking cookies, no analytics, no ads, no third-party trackers. The web host sees the usual connection details (IP address, page requested) in its logs, like any website; we don't use them to identify or profile anyone.

The My Phone app

The app talks only to the PBX you sign into. It doesn't send your calls, messages, voicemail, contacts or recordings to OwnPBX.org, Google or anyone else. It doesn't read your phone's address book, call log or messages. The microphone is used only during calls, push-to-talk and recording a greeting; the camera only to scan a sign-in QR code. A crash report, if the app crashes, stays on your phone unless you choose to send it.

Your PBX account

An account holds what it needs to work: your name, extension and email address, your messages (including SMS/MMS), voicemails and greeting, call history (who called whom, when and for how long), call recordings if you turn recording on, faxes, contacts and notes, and your settings. If 911 is set up for your account, it also holds the street address registered for 911. The server keeps a log of sign-ins (time and IP address) to protect your account.

2. Calls, texts, 911 and fax through a VoIP provider

In plain language: calls and messages between people on the PBX stay on the PBX. Anything that reaches a regular phone number goes through a VoIP provider (we use voip.ms), which sees what it needs to deliver it.

Calls, messages, voicemail and push-to-talk between extensions are handled entirely by the PBX and are never sent to a provider.

Calls to and from regular phone numbers, SMS and MMS, 911 and faxes are carried by the VoIP provider the PBX administrator connects. To deliver them, the provider receives the phone numbers involved, the times, the call audio, the text of SMS/MMS messages and their pictures, the fax pages, and for 911 the registered street address. The provider handles that data under its own privacy policy. Passing a call or message to a provider to complete it is how phone service works; it isn't a sale of your data.

3. What we never collect

  • No advertising, advertising ID or advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or cross-site tracking.
  • No location tracking. The app doesn't use GPS. The only address we hold is the 911 address, if one is set up, and it's used only for 911.
  • No Google services in the app, and nothing shared with Google, Meta or data brokers.
  • We don't sell personal information or share it for advertising.

4. Where your data lives

In plain language: there's no OwnPBX cloud. Your data lives on the PBX you use, and whoever runs that PBX is responsible for it.

OwnPBX is software that runs on a server someone controls. For accounts on myaccount.ownpbx.org, the OwnPBX project operator runs the server and this policy applies. If you use a PBX that you or someone else runs, that server's administrator is responsible for your data, and requests go to them; this policy still describes what the software does.

5. Get a copy of your data, or delete your account

In plain language: sign in at myaccount.ownpbx.org/ucp/delete. A copy of your data is emailed to you as a download link. A deleted account is gone within 90 days.

How to ask

  • Online (fastest): sign in at myaccount.ownpbx.org/ucp/delete. It's also in My Phone under Settings, and in the app under Settings → Account.
  • Can't sign in? Email us from the address on your account, with your extension and "Delete my account" or "Copy of my data" in the subject, or write to us (section 10).

A copy of your data

You get an email with a download link to a zip file. It holds your account details and settings, call history, messages (SMS, MMS and internal), voicemails and your greeting, call recordings, faxes, contacts and notes. Passwords and security keys aren't included. The link works for 7 days and doesn't need a sign-in, so keep it private. Requests are answered within 30 days, normally much sooner.

Deleting your account

You get an email confirming the request and the date it will be done, which is within 90 days. You can cancel until then by signing in again. Once deleted, your login, extension, call history, messages, voicemails, recordings, faxes, contacts and settings are removed from the server, and you get a confirmation email. Your extension and phone number may later be given to someone else.

Some things are kept: a record that your request was made and completed (your username, email address and dates), and billing records the law requires us to keep. Messages, recordings or faxes already delivered to other people stay with them, and your VoIP provider keeps its own records under its own policy.

6. California privacy rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, California residents can ask to know what personal information we hold and why, to have it deleted or corrected, and to opt out of its sale or sharing (we don't sell or share it, so there's nothing to opt out of). Using these rights won't change how you're treated. We confirm a request within 10 business days and complete it within 45 days, or tell you if we need up to 45 more days (never more than 90 in total). To protect you, a request must come from your signed-in account or your account's email address.

7. Security and how long data is kept

The web panel is protected by your password (or single sign-on), and the app by a one-time sign-in plus a PIN you choose. Calls from outside your home network are encrypted (TLS and SRTP). Only the administrator can reach the server. No system is perfectly secure: if you think your account has been misused, tell us right away and we'll lock it and change its keys.

Data is kept while your account exists, and you can delete voicemails, recordings and messages yourself at any time. Deleted data is removed from the live server; backups, if any, age out on their normal cycle and are never used to bring back a deleted account.

8. Children

Accounts are created only for invited users. OwnPBX isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child's information has been provided, contact us and it will be deleted.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects your rights, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and account holders will be told by email or in My Phone.
