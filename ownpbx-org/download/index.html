Download My Phone

My Phone is the Android client for your PBX: calls, texts, voicemail, and push-to-talk in one app. It is free, open-source software (GPL-2.0-or-later).

Before you install

  • You need a PBX account. The app connects to a PBX server you run or administer. Accounts are created by the PBX administrator — there is no signup inside the app, and if you are not authorized on a PBX, you cannot log in.
  • Android only, for now. The app is built for Android phones. It contains no Google services and no tracking.
  • Signing in. Open the app, confirm the Server address (your PBX address), tap "Sign in with my browser", sign in with your extension number and password, and choose a PIN. Sign-in lasts 60 days after last use.
  • Emergency calls. Do not rely on this app for 911 — emergency calling has not been tested end to end. Use a regular phone for emergencies. See Terms of Service, section 5.

Your data, your rights

The app talks only to your PBX server. You can ask your administrator to delete your account or send you a download of your data — voicemails, SMS, MMS, messages, and call records — within 90 days. Full details: Privacy Policy.
