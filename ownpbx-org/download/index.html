Download My Phone

My Phone is the Android app for your PBX: calls, messages, voicemail and push-to-talk in one place. Free, open-source software (GPL-2.0-or-later).

Get the app

Download the latest release (GitHub) COMING SOON ONGoogle Play

Google Play is coming soon; until then, GitHub has every release. The GitHub version checks for updates itself and asks before installing one.

Before you install

  • You need an account on a PBX. The app connects to a PBX you run or have been invited to. There's no signup in the app.
  • Signing in. Open the app, check the Server address, tap "Sign in with my browser" and sign in. The app sets up your phone line by itself. Sign-in lasts 60 days after you last use the app.
  • Outside calls need a VoIP provider. Calls and messages between people on your PBX are free. Regular phone numbers, SMS/MMS, 911 and fax only work if your PBX has a VoIP provider (OwnPBX is tested with voip.ms; see the home page).
  • Don't rely on it for 911. Emergency calling hasn't been tested end to end. Keep a regular phone for emergencies (Terms, section 5).
  • Android only, for now. No Google services, no ads, no tracking.

Your data

The app talks only to your PBX. Ask for a copy of your data or delete your account at myaccount.ownpbx.org/ucp/delete (or with your own PBX's administrator). Details in the Privacy Policy.
