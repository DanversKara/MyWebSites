2. What OwnPBX is

In plain language: OwnPBX is free software, not a phone company. On its own it connects the people on a PBX to each other. Regular phone numbers need a VoIP provider.

OwnPBX is free, open-source software (GPL-2.0-or-later) for running your own phone system. By itself it provides calls, messages, voicemail and push-to-talk between users of the same PBX, over the internet, at no cost. OwnPBX is not a carrier and doesn't provide phone numbers.

Calls to and from regular phone numbers, SMS/MMS, 911 and fax only work when the PBX administrator connects a third-party VoIP provider. OwnPBX is tested with voip.ms; other providers may work but aren't tested. The provider's own terms, prices and rules apply to that service, and OwnPBX doesn't guarantee it.

5. Emergency calling (911 / E911): read this

In plain language: don't count on OwnPBX for 911. Keep a regular mobile phone or landline for emergencies.

911 only works if the PBX has a VoIP provider with 911 service and a correct street address registered for the number used. It has not been tested end to end and must not be relied on. A 911 call can fail, or reach emergency services without your correct location, if the server, the internet connection, the provider or the power is down, or if the registered address is out of date. A PBX with no VoIP provider has no 911 at all. By using OwnPBX you accept these limits.
