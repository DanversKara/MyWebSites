Eligibility & How It Works

AirTalk Wireless participates in the federal Lifeline program (and California LifeLine, where applicable), which provides a free or discounted phone and monthly wireless service to qualifying low-income households. You may qualify based on participation in a program like Medicaid, SNAP, SSI, or Federal Public Housing Assistance, or based on household income. ✅
