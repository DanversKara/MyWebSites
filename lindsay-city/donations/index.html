Why this exists

This is a passion project, run out of pocket.

Lindsay.City started because Lindsay deserved a place online that felt like it — the groves, the events, the everyday life of the city — kept up by someone who actually lives here. It isn't a business and it isn't affiliated with the City of Lindsay. It's maintained with personal time, personal equipment, and money out of my own pocket for hosting and software.

If the site has ever been useful or fun for you, a donation helps keep it online. It's completely optional — nothing on Lindsay.City is ever locked behind a donation.

Where it goes

What your donation actually supports

  • 🌐Website hosting, domain, and infrastructure
  • 🎥Equipment for photos and video
  • 🧰Software and services that keep the site running
  • 👷Ongoing development and maintenance
  • ✍️New content — pages, photos, videos, event coverage
  • ⏱️The time it takes to run the whole thing
The thank-you

What donors get in return

Every donation is voluntary, and any recognition is Lindsay.City's way of saying thanks — not something you're buying. Depending on the level and what's available, a thank-you placement might include:

  • 🏳️A website banner in an available spot
  • 🔗A linked logo, brand, or website
  • 🏷️Your name, channel, or username on the page
  • 📣A social-media link
  • 🎬A mention or thank-you in a Lindsay.City video
🎁 Donate → 📅 28 days of recognition → 🔄 Placement ends → 🍊 Donate again for another 28 days

There's no auto-renewal and no obligation to come back — every recognition period simply runs its course after 28 days.

The honest fine print

A few things worth knowing

Not a purchase

You're not buying an ad package — no guaranteed views, clicks, or followers come with a donation.

Not tax-deductible

Lindsay.City isn't a 501(c)(3). Talk to a tax professional if you have questions about a payment.

Independent of the City

Lindsay.City is not the official City of Lindsay website and isn't run or endorsed by the City government.

No business required

Individuals, creators, streamers, and organizations are all welcome to donate — a business isn't necessary.

Your call on your name

Your brand, channel, or handle is used by default. Your personal name is only used if you ask for it.

Everything's subject to change

If a placement isn't available, or the site gets redesigned, Lindsay.City will find a comparable spot instead.

Include:

  • The name, brand, channel, or username to display
  • The website or profile you'd like linked
  • Your donation level
  • A preferred placement, if you have one
  • Any logo, photo, graphic, or video clip to consider

Media specs: please keep images to 2000px max on the longest side and under 2MB. Placements are cropped to fit their spot, so a logo shaped to roughly match your chosen placement (wide banner vs. tall sidebar vs. square icon) will look best — sending something like 6000×6000 just gets downscaled and slows down your upload for no benefit.

Option B: Prefer to donate without recognition?

You can also make a donation without requesting any website banner, page placement, linked logo, or video mention. If you choose this option, your donation will be treated as support-only, and you voluntarily waive any request for donor recognition associated with the donation.

There is no advertising, placement, or video mention included with a support-only donation.
