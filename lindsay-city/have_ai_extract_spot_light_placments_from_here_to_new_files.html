About Lindsay, CA 93247

🍊 Citrus groves & community events 🌸

Lindsay is a small city in Tulare County, California, known for its citrus groves, the annual Lindsay Orange Blossom Festival, and community events like the Lindsay Farmers Market.

This site is an independent, resident-run project — separate from the official City of Lindsay government — sharing on-the-ground video and photo documentation from around the 93247 area.
