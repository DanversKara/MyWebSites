Download the apps BearerMail addition

BearerMail has an app for Android phones and tablets, and one for iPhone and iPad. Both connect to your own BearerMail server and nothing else.

⬇ Android →

The newest BearerMail for Android, always. Works on every Android phone, with or without Google.

⬇ iPhone & iPad →

The iOS app, and the three ways Apple lets you install it.

On a computer →

Nothing to install: open your BearerMail address in any browser.

Android

⬇ Download BearerMail for Android — the newest version; the link follows every release.

  • Open the file on the phone and allow the install when Android asks. Then pair it — see Android app → Pairing.
  • Want new mail to arrive through Google's push service? Take the Google version instead (com.bearermail.mail.google). Same app otherwise; it installs next to the standard one.
  • Every version with its notes: all Android releases.

Once installed, the app updates itself — it checks for a newer release and offers it.

iPhone & iPad

⬇ BearerMail for iPhone and iPad

Apple only lets apps onto an iPhone through the App Store or as an app signed for that phone, so it takes a few more steps than Android. Everything is on the iPhone & iPad page.

In a hurry?

Open your BearerMail address in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. The web app opens full screen like an app, with notifications on iOS 16.4 and newer.

"Install blocked" on Android

Android asks once for each app that opens an APK — your browser or file manager. Allow it for that app, then tap the file again.
