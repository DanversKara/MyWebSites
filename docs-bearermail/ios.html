iPhone & iPad BearerMail addition

The BearerMail app for iPhone and iPad. Like the Android app it talks only to your own server.

Not in the App Store

Apple allows three ways to put an app on an iPhone that is not in the App Store. Pick one below. Downloads: iOS releases.

WayYou needLasts
Web app on the Home ScreenSafariFor good — nothing to renew
SideStore or AltStore with the .ipaA free Apple ID7 days, then renews itself (SideStore) or from your computer (AltStore)
Build it with XcodeA Mac with Xcode 26, a free Apple ID7 days, then build again

What the app does

The same BearerMail as on Android and the web, in the iPhone's own look:

  • Mail and calendar — the full plMail mail client and calendar.
  • Notes — the notes wall (Notes / Archive / Bin, pinned first, coloured cards, labels, search), an editor that saves by itself, checklists, reminders, pictures and voice notes, and Share. A mail's ••• menu has Save as note.
  • Contacts — A–Z list with search and tags, tap to mail, call or map, photos, recent mail with each person, editing, and Find duplicates with merge and undo.
  • Drive — folders and files, picture previews, open anything in Quick Look, copy share links, upload photos or files, storage used.
  • Composer — Insert note and Insert Drive link in the attach menu; a file too big to attach goes to Drive as a link automatically.
  • Aliases — Settings → Sending addresses → New alias, typed or random.
  • App lock — Settings → App lock: Face ID, Touch ID or passcode, and mail hidden in the app switcher.

Not on iPhone (they need a paid Apple developer account): push notifications while the app is closed, and Home Screen widgets.

Web app on the Home Screen

  1. Open your BearerMail address in Safari and sign in.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open it from the Home Screen and allow notifications when asked (iOS 16.4+).

SideStore or AltStore

  1. Install SideStore or AltStore following their own guide.
  2. On the iPhone, download BearerMail-iOS.ipa from the latest iOS release.
  3. Open it with SideStore or AltStore and sign in with your Apple ID. The app is signed for your phone only.

Build it with Xcode

Step by step in the repo: Putting BearerMail on your own iPhone or iPad.

Pairing

On the server: Settings → App passwords → Pair a device, then scan the code with the app.

Things that bite

"Untrusted Developer"

After a SideStore, AltStore or Xcode install: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust.

Stops opening after a week

Apple's limit for apps signed with a free Apple ID. Refresh in SideStore/AltStore, or build again. Only a paid Apple developer account (or the App Store) removes it.

No notifications while closed, empty widget

With a free Apple ID, Apple grants neither push nor the widget's shared data. The Home Screen web app does get notifications.
