BearerMail for Android — Terms of Service

For the BearerMail Android app (com.bearermail.mail, and the com.bearermail.mail.google flavour), the free, open-source companion app for a BearerMail / plMail server. Last updated October 27, 2026.

In short

The app is free, open-source software, provided as-is with no warranty. It is a companion app for a mail server you (or your administrator) run — the developer runs no mail service, holds none of your mail, and cannot recover an account on somebody else’s server.

What the app is

BearerMail for Android is developed by the BearerMail project (DanversKara/pl_mail_android_bearermail), published as free software. There is no company behind it. The developer runs no hosted service for the app: the app talks only to the mail server you configure, and that server is yours, or your administrator’s — not the developer’s.

What you need to use it

The app is useless on its own: it needs a mail server to connect to, and an account on that server. You are responsible for keeping your sign-in details safe. If the administrator of your server removes or suspends your account, the app stops working for you — that is expected, and it is the administrator’s decision, not the developer’s. Account recovery, password resets, and storage limits are all the server administrator’s job.

No warranty

The app is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind — including any promise that it will work, keep working, or be fit for any particular purpose. You use it at your own risk. The developer does not promise that mail will be delivered, that nothing will be lost, or that any particular server will stay online: delivery and retention depend on the server you connect to and the providers it uses.

Your mail lives on your server

Mail, folders, contacts, calendar entries, notes, drive files, and aliases live on your mail server, under whoever runs that server. What that server keeps, for how long, and who else can see it is decided there, not by this app and not by these terms. If you use a server somebody else runs, their rules apply to your account on top of these terms.

Acceptable use

Do not use the app to send spam, to harass anyone, to impersonate someone, to phish, or to break the law. Mail you send is subject to your mail server’s policies and to the delivery providers that server uses. Abuse of a server you do not run is between you and that server’s administrator.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the new version will be posted on this page, with a new “last updated” date.
